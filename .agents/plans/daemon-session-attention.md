# Serve one daemon-owned session attention state

Maintain this living ExecPlan according to `.agents/PLANS.md`.

## Purpose / Big Picture

Every session surface must show the daemon's answer to whether a person is needed. NeedsInput uses ?, Important uses !, failures and unreachable workers use ×, and locally unread replies use ✓. ASCII equivalents are ?, !, x, and +. Idle and working remain activity colours. A child's open elicitation promotes its parent in the daemon, including ancestors. The daemon never stores read receipts.

It explicitly includes reviewer questions in session.pending_elicitations. This is richer than owner.publish_view alone; do not compute two independent classifications at those sites. Find their common publication boundary or feed one computed map to both.
