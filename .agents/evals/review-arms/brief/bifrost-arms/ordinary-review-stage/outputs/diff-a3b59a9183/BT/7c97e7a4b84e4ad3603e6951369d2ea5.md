- [P1] Preserve complete-empty implicit types in hierarchy traversal — crates/bifrost-analysis/src/analyzer/resolution/selected_context.rs:1569-1570. When an unqualified Java supertype such as `Exception` has a complete-empty scope answer and is registered as an implicit fallback, `cached_hierarchy_reference_parts` now ignores its identity and returns the empty answer as complete. Consequently the superclass and its inherited members disappear from hierarchy traversal, even when a durable external owner and its inventory are available. The fallback needs to remain available to hierarchy lookup after verifying that the selected answer is complete and empty.
