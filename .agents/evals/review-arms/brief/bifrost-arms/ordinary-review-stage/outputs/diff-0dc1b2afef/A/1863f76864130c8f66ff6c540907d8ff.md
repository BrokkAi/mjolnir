- [P2] Preserve earlier-namespace uncertainty when selecting a method value — crates/bifrost-analysis/src/analyzer/resolution/fact_resolution.rs:27041-27042
  When the earlier value lookup is incomplete, for example because a qualifier or member scope could not be resolved, `replayed` carries that uncertainty and `deferred_completion` may carry additional uncertainty from the ordinary impl lookup. Returning only `direct` and `members` discards both, so a later method value can be reported as a complete binding even though an earlier value may still shadow it. Preserve the earlier lookup's completion in the selected answer.
