- [P1] Unregister the trace from the reader that owns it — crates/bifrost-analysis/src/analyzer/store/resolution_operation_tests.rs:593-597
  When a point measurement finishes, the selected operation still holds the traced reader, so `finish_production_selected_sql_trace` checks out a different reader and unregisters its callback instead. These lines then free the context while the original reader still has its callback installed; SQL executed during its cleanup or a later checkout dereferences freed memory. This affects both the new Go measurement and the existing Rust measurement and can cause undefined behavior or crashes.
