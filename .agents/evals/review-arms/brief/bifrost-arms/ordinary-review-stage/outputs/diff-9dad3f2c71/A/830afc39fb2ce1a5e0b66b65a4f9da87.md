- [P1] Release the traced reader before freeing its callback context — crates/bifrost-analysis/src/analyzer/store/resolution_operation_tests.rs:3741
  The Go measurement calls `finish_production_selected_sql_trace` while `operation` still owns the traced reader through its mount inventory. The finish helper therefore checks out a different pooled connection, unregisters its callback, and frees the context while the original connection still points to it. When that reader is reused by the next measurement, SQLite invokes the callback with a dangling pointer, causing undefined behavior or a crash.
