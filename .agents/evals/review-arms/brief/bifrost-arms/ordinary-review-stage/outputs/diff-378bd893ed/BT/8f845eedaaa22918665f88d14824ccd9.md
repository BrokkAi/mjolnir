- [P2] Propagate the semantic request's cancellation and budget to the census — crates/bifrost-analysis/src/analyzer/go/package_function_value.rs:105-105. When a package-function call is resolved in a large workspace, this invokes the public usage lookup with a fresh cancellation token and its own default limits, independently of the enclosing semantic request. Cancelling the request during the scan therefore cannot stop it, and the scan can read and process many files despite an exhausted or small semantic source/traversal budget; repeated calls can make a bounded analysis take substantially longer than requested. Pass the request's cancellation and remaining limits through the usage query and account for its work.
