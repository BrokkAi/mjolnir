- [P2] Represent unavailable witness evidence as unknown, not unproven — crates/bifrost-rql/src/structural/search/witness_projection.rs:75-79. When witness retrieval fails, this default is returned with an unavailable reason even though no witness was inspected. The published `proof: "unproven"` and `completeness: "partial"` therefore assert outcomes the analysis could not establish; the requested evidence contract requires unavailable dimensions to be null. Consumers cannot distinguish a genuinely unproven partial witness from one whose evidence could not be retrieved.
