- [P2] Avoid cloning cached support completions without polling — crates/bifrost-analysis/src/analyzer/resolution/fact_resolution.rs:14253-14257. When a hierarchy support identity is encountered again with a large incomplete completion, `.cloned()` now copies its entire reason array before the cancellation ledger can be polled; previously this cloned only an `Rc`.
