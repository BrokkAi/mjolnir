- [P2] Avoid cloning entire cached support completions on replay — crates/bifrost-analysis/src/analyzer/resolution/fact_resolution.rs:4677. When a hierarchy support identity is read again with a large incomplete completion, `publish_hierarchy_support_completion` still calls `.cloned()` on the cache entry before its polling comparison. This change turns that formerly cheap handle clone into an unpolled allocation and copy of every reason, followed by another full traversal and drop. Large completions can therefore delay cancellation and add substantial repeated work on cached reads; compare the retained reasons by key under polling instead.
