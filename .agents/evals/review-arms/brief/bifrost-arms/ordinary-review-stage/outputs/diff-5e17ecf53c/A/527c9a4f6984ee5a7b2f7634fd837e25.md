- [P2] Stop recovery at the end of a completed macro — src/analyzer/usages/get_definition/cpp.rs:3965-3966
  When an ordinary function-like macro is followed immediately by an unrelated malformed declaration or expression, an identifier inside that wrapper's `ERROR` node is now treated as part of the macro tail. A `preproc_function_def` with a value does not establish that recovery continued beyond its end, so definition lookup incorrectly reports `declaration_or_import_site` for references in incomplete code; the clean-declaration boundary test does not cover this adjacent case.
