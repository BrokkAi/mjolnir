- [P1] Preserve unproven member-family results when discharging dispatch gaps — crates/bifrost-analysis/src/analyzer/semantic/workspace_oracle/dispatch.rs:553-557. When the member-family walk is incomplete, such as when overload identity cannot be established or its visit budget is exhausted, `virtual_dispatch_implementor_targets` returns `Some(Vec::new())` rather than `None`. This branch therefore treats an unproven hierarchy as proof that no overrides exist and removes the dynamic-dispatch gap; a real override can be omitted while the call and its value-flow snapshot are reported complete, producing false negatives. The family outcome must remain distinguishable from a proven empty set.
