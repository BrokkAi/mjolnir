- [P1] Invalidate conditional writes when their outcome cannot be tracked — crates/bifrost-rql/src/structural/search/effects.rs:2677-2695: When a modeled call's result is ignored, assigned to a variable, or otherwise lacks a direct opaque guard, the call is still marked as having complete address coverage, but its conditional write is never applied. For example, after `var e *exec.ExitError; errors.As(err, &e)`, `e` remains classified as definitely nil even though a successful call can populate it, so a subsequent `e.ExitCode()` produces a false positive. The target must be conservatively invalidated whenever the modeled outcome cannot be attached to an edge.
