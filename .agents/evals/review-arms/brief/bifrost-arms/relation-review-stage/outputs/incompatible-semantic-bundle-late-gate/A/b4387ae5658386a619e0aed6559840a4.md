- [P2] Wait for an in-flight acquisition before suppressing another attempt — crates/bifrost-semantic-packs/src/download.rs:86-88

  When two workspaces sharing a catalog concurrently miss on different production keys, the first marks the catalog as attempted before its download and installation finish. The second immediately falls back to local generation, even if the in-flight bundle contains its exact JDK production; the production locks are key-specific, so they do not serialize these calls. This can reproduce the expensive cold build the acquisition is intended to avoid. Track an in-progress attempt and let concurrent callers recheck the catalog after it completes.
