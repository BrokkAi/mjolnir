- [P2] Wait for an in-flight download before suppressing another attempt — crates/bifrost-semantic-packs/src/download.rs:84-86. When two threads prepare different dependency keys against the same catalog concurrently, the first claims the catalog and starts downloading while the second immediately receives an error and falls back to local production. If the second key is the matching JDK production, it can perform the expensive build that the in-flight bundle would have avoided; production locks are key-specific and therefore do not serialize these calls. The shared attempt needs to expose its completed outcome to other callers rather than treating an in-progress attempt as a completed failure.
