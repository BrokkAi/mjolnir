- [P1] Avoid scanning the entire cache on every persistence batch — src/analyzer/store/mod.rs:4751-4756. When a large cache contains few or no stale blobs, this inequality against the joined epoch requires walking the blob registry to discover that there is nothing to reclaim. `try_persist_prepared_chunk` invokes this maintenance synchronously after every committed batch, while holding the store mutex and an immediate write transaction, so a fresh or fully reclaimed cache repeatedly scans its growing registry and blocks other writers. The 10,000-row deletion budget does not bound this scan, making large refreshes potentially quadratic in the number of batches.
